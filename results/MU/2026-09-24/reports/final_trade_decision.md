**Investment Thesis**: The bull case carried the debate on substance. The fundamentals are audited and independently verifiable: $41.46B quarterly revenue up 345% YoY, 84.6% gross margin, $17.56B quarterly FCF, net cash of ~$18.6B after cutting debt from $16.1B to $6.4B, ROE 66.6%, and finished-goods inventory falling from $1.22B to $0.62B while revenue nearly tripled — the signature of a genuine shortage, not a channel-stuffing top. The bear case rests almost entirely on analogy to 2018/2022 cycles; when pressed, no forward-looking datapoint of deterioration exists today (no glut, no collapsing DSO, no cancelled contracts), and flat DSO at ~59 days across two explosive quarters defeats the receivables accusation on arithmetic. The structural changes the bear never adequately rebutted: multiyear deposit-backed contracts running toward decade-end (the TSMC-ification of memory pricing), DRAM revenue +67% on flat shipments showing pure pricing power, and a three-player qualified HBM oligopoly where CXMT has no demonstrated HBM capability. The bear's best points — that low forward P/E of 6.7 on peak earnings is the classic cyclical-top signature, and that capex rising from $2.9B to $7.8B/quarter plus the 11,000-contract call block expiring after earnings create gamma that amplifies whichever way the print breaks — were legitimate and were absorbed as sizing discipline rather than a veto. What the bear won is the setup into the print, not the direction: price 64% above the 200 SMA, RSI ~62 approaching overbought, target raises that stopped lifting the stock, and 2.22 beta meaning a merely in-line print could draw down 20-30% — and a stop does not bound an earnings gap, which is why the pre-print footprint is small and gap-sized. What the bull won is the asymmetry: the stock at $1,071.88 trades ~18% below the consensus target zone and ~3% below Citi's $1,300, undermining the negative-skew claim (expectations are not priced above the tape), and even on a 40%-compressed EPS of ~$95 the stock trades ~11x with a net-cash balance sheet. The neutral's gap-symmetry point was decisive on sequencing: if a bad print can gap down 25%, waiting for confirmation means paying $1,250+ for information available next week. Hence Buy with staged execution — small pre-print tranche, post-print adds on validation, $950 invalidation, and gates binding in both directions (exit immediately on a 10+ day DSO blowout, receivables materially outgrowing revenue QoQ, or finished-goods inflecting higher; a capex raise without corresponding contract/demand disclosure would also flip this to Underweight).
