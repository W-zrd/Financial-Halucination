## 1. Company Profile

Micron Technology, Inc. is a global leader in semiconductor memory and storage solutions, producing DRAM, NAND flash, and NOR flash memory products under the Micron and Crucial brands. Its products serve data centers, AI infrastructure, PC/mobile OEMs, automotive, and industrial markets. The company is one of the "Big 3" memory makers (with Samsung and SK Hynix) and is a primary beneficiary of the AI-driven HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) demand cycle.

## 5. Valuation & Risk Assessment

**Valuation:**
- **PE (TTM):** 24.75 — reasonable even at cycle-peak earnings
- **Forward PE: 6.74** — exceptionally low; market pricing in meaningful earnings normalization
- **PEG: 0.16** — deeply undervalued on a growth-adjusted basis
- **Price/Book: 12.0** — rich on book value, though ROE of 66.6% and ROA of 34.9% justify a premium
- **FTM EV considerations:** Net cash position further depresses enterprise value relative to peers

**Bull case:** AI/HBM demand remains structurally supply-constrained; record margins and FCF; net cash balance sheet; forward PE < 7 with forward EPS consensus of ~$159; lean inventory; stock 12% below 50-day average may offer entry after consolidation.

**Bear case / risks:**
1. **Memory is historically cyclical** — gross margins above 80% are unheard of historically and invite competitor capacity expansion and demand destruction; the forward PE of 6.7 is the market's implicit warning.
2. **Receivables growth (+$21.4B in 12 months) far outpacing revenue** — potential collection/timing risk; a reversal would hit cash flow hard.
3. **Rising capex** (19% of revenue and climbing) creates fixed-cost leverage that amplifies any downturn.
4. **High beta (2.22)** — expect sharp drawdowns; the ~$1,255 52-week high vs. current levels shows volatility is real.
5. Vendor fundamentals show a **FCF discrepancy** ($7.6B stated vs. ~$26.2B summed from quarterly statements) — verify against official 10-K/10-Q filings.

**Actionable insights for traders:**
- **Fundamental momentum is overwhelmingly positive** — record revenue, margins, EPS, and FCF with a net-cash balance sheet. Fundamentals support ownership.
- **Entry discipline matters:** Given beta 2.22 and cyclical history, scale in rather than buy full size; the 50-day average (~$932) and 200-day average (~$648) are key technical support zones.
- **Next catalysts:** FQ4'26 earnings (fiscal year-end August 2026, reported late September 2026) — watch receivables/DSO, forward guidance on HBM pricing, capex plans, and any buyback resumption announcement.
- **Key risk trigger to exit/trim:** sequential revenue or gross-margin decline, or a sharp reversal in accounts receivable.
