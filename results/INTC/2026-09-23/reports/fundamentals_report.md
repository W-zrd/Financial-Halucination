## 2. Executive Summary

Intel's Q2 FY2026 (ended 2026-06-30) shows a **clear operational inflection** masked by an enormous one-time investment loss:

- **Revenue $16.13B, +25.4% YoY** (vs. $12.86B in Q2 FY25) and **+18.8% QoQ** — the strongest top line in the available dataset.
- **Gross margin 40.4%** vs. 27.5% a year ago (**+12.8pp YoY**) — best quarterly gross margin in the 5-quarter window, approaching historical norms (42.6% in FY2022).
- **Operating income +$1.97B** (12.2% margin) vs. **–$1.29B** (–10.0%) a year ago — fourth consecutive positive operating-income quarter and accelerating.
- **Operating cash flow $7.01B** (best quarter in the dataset) and **free cash flow +$4.45B** — Intel's first meaningfully FCF-positive quarter in years; **trailing-twelve-month FCF is +$2.83B**, the first positive TTM FCF in the 2022–2026 window (FY2022–FY2025 FCF: –$9.4B, –$14.3B, –$15.7B, –$4.9B).
- **GAAP net loss –$11.03B (diluted EPS –$2.16)** — but this was driven by **–$12.74B of pre-tax unusual items**, dominated by a **–$12.48B loss on sale of a security/investment** (cash-flow statement shows a ~$12.4B non-cash add-back plus a **$4.22B asset impairment charge**). Excluding unusual items, pre-tax income was approximately **+$1.92B** vs. –$1.48B in Q2 FY25.
- **Balance sheet caution:** In Q2 2026 Intel issued **$13.0B of new debt** (repaid $8.33B), showed a **–$12.01B "other financing" cash outflow**, and equity fell **–$23.85B QoQ** to $87.5B; **net debt rose to $37.66B** (from $32.32B at YE2025). Liquidity remains adequate ($29.7B cash + short-term investments; current ratio 1.60x).

**Bottom line for traders:** the operating turnaround is real and measurable (revenue, margin, EBITDA, OCF, FCF all inflecting), but GAAP results remain polluted by large one-time charges, the balance sheet weakened in Q2 2026, and 2025's recapitalization diluted holders ~15%. The swing factors into the Q3 FY2026 print are margin sustainability ≥40%, FCF confirmation, and clarification of the Q2 security-loss/financing-outflow transaction.

## 3. Company Profile

- **Company:** Intel Corporation (**INTC**), listed on NMS (Nasdaq).
- **Business classification:** Technology / Semiconductors. Intel is an integrated device manufacturer and merchant foundry operator: client/data-center CPUs, accelerators, and Intel Foundry services (leading-edge fab network).
- **Scale (Q2 2026):** Total assets **$202.4B**, net PP&E **$105.7B**, revenue run-rate ~$64B, ~5.04B shares outstanding.
- **Structural notes visible in the statements:**
  - **Minority interest has grown sharply:** $1.86B (YE2022) → $5.76B (YE2024) → $12.08B (YE2025) → **$15.60B (Q2 2026)**, reflecting consolidation of partially divested businesses (notably the **51% Altera stake sale in Q3 2025**, which produced a **+$5.55B gain on sale of business** that quarter) and public minority floats (e.g., Mobileye).
  - **No dividend** has been paid since FY2024 (FY2025–H1 2026 dividends = $0; FY2022 dividend was $6.0B, FY2023 $3.1B, FY2024 $1.6B). **No buybacks** since 2021.
  - **Equity recapitalization in 2025:** net common stock issuance of **+$12.7B** in FY2025 (strategic/government-type investments; ~$13.5B of "stock proceeds" inflows per the vendor's financing detail), lifting share count from 4.33B (YE2024) to 4.99B (YE2025) — **~15% dilution concentrated in H2 2025**. Dilution slowed markedly in 2026: only +49M shares (+1.0%) in H1 2026.

## 9. Strengths / Risks / Watch Items

**Strengths (evidence-based):**
1. Revenue growth inflection: +25.4% YoY, +18.8% QoQ in Q2 FY26; TTM revenue $57.0B, +7.9% vs. FY2025.
2. Gross margin 40.4% and rising for four of five quarters; still ~2–15pp below Intel's historical mid-40s–50s norms → further recovery runway if product mix/foundry yields hold.
3. Five consecutive quarters of improving normalized EBITDA; four consecutive positive operating-income quarters.
4. OCF $7.0B and FCF +$4.45B in Q2 FY26; **TTM FCF +$2.83B — first positive TTM FCF since at least FY2021** in this dataset.
5. Cost base reset: opex $4.54B/quarter vs. $4.83B a year ago; FY25 opex 25% below FY22.
6. Dilution decelerated to ~1% in H1 2026 after the 2025 recapitalization.
7. Deep asset base and liquidity: $105.7B net PP&E, $29.7B liquid assets, TBV $12.80/share.

**Risks:**
1. **Unexplained Q2 FY26 transaction:** –$12.5B security loss + –$12.0B financing outflow + –$12.5B common-stock account decline. Until the 10-Q clarifies, capital-allocation risk is elevated.
2. **Leverage creep:** net debt +$9.9B QoQ to $37.7B; $13B new debt issued; interest expense +41% YoY.
3. **Equity erosion:** stockholders' equity –$26.7B since YE2025 to $87.5B; retained earnings down to $33.8B.
4. **GAAP losses persist** (–$14.8B in H1 FY26) and the tax shield appears limited.
5. **Under-investment signal:** Q2 FY26 capex ($2.56B) **below D&A ($3.22B)** — supportive of near-term FCF but a potential long-term competitiveness concern for a leading-edge foundry ramp (unless offset by government incentives/SCIP partner funding, which the data suggests — e.g., FY2024's +$11.1B "other financing" inflow consistent with a fab co-investment).
6. Inventory ~118 days ($12.5B) — obsolescence risk; goodwill $20.5B residual impairment risk; $8.4B AFS securities marked to market.
7. No dividend, no buyback — total-return case depends entirely on operational recovery.

**Watch items for the next print (Q3 FY2026, ~late Oct 2026):** revenue ≥ $14B (sustaining growth), gross margin ≥ 40%, positive FCF, opex ≤ $4.6B, net debt trajectory, share count, disclosure of the Q2 security-loss transaction, inventory days, capex vs. D&A.

## 10. Actionable Insights for Traders

1. **Separate the signal from the noise:** the tradeable fundamental signal is the **operating inflection** (revenue +25% YoY, GM 40.4%, op income +$1.97B, FCF +$4.45B, TTM FCF +$2.83B). The –$11.0B GAAP loss is dominated by a one-time, largely non-cash investment loss; underneath it, ex-unusual pretax income was +$1.9B — a ~$3.3B YoY swing. Positioned long, the thesis is "GAAP loss, economic profit"; positioned short/defensive, the thesis is "equity –$26.7B in two quarters, net debt +$9.9B QoQ."
2. **Key catalyst within ~5 weeks:** Q3 FY2026 earnings. Confirmation checklist: revenue ≥$14B, GM ≥40%, FCF >0, no new large unusual items. A second consecutive quarter of ≥$4B FCF would validate the multi-year FCF-burn reversal (FY22–FY25 cumulative FCF burn –$44.3B).
3. **Fundamental anchors for risk framing (not price targets):** tangible book value **$12.80/share**, book value **$17.36/share**, net PP&E **$20.97/share**, cash+ST investments **$5.90/share**, net debt **$7.47/share**. These are the balance-sheet reference points a market-data agent can compare against the current quote.
4. **Balance-sheet risk is now the swing factor:** 2.1x net leverage on normalized EBITDA is manageable, but two more quarters like Q2 FY26 (–$12B financing outflows) would pressure the story. Track the 10-Q explanation of the security loss and whether it settled a contingent obligation (de-risking) or destroyed value.
5. **Dilution risk is fading:** +1.0% share growth in H1 2026 vs. +15.3% in 2025 — per-share improvements now flow through more directly.
6. **Margin runway:** at 40.4% GM vs. 42.6% in FY2022 and mid-50s historical norms, every +1pp of GM on a $64B run-rate adds ~$0.6B of annual gross profit; with opex at ~$18B/yr, the operating-margin torque is high.
7. **What would falsify the recovery:** GM back below ~36%, revenue below ~$13.5B, FCF negative, or another multi-billion write-down — any of these in the Q3 print would reset the turnaround narrative.
