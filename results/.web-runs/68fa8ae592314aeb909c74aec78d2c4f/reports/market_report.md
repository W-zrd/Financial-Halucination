### Technical Indicator Selection & Rationale

To analyze **Microsoft Corporation (MSFT)** effectively as of **September 25, 2026**, eight complementary indicators were selected to avoid redundancy while covering trend direction, momentum strength, volatility, and volume-weighted price structure:

1. **`close_10_ema` (10-day Exponential Moving Average):** Captures short-term momentum shifts and dynamic tactical support/resistance.
2. **`close_50_sma` (50-day Simple Moving Average):** Establishes medium-term trend direction and structural support.
3. **`close_200_sma` (200-day Simple Moving Average):** Benchmark for primary long-term market trend alignment.
4. **`rsi` (Relative Strength Index):** Measures velocity and magnitude of price changes to assess overbought/oversold levels and momentum divergence.
5. **`macd` (MACD Line):** Evaluates overall momentum differential between short-term (12-day) and longer-term (26-day) EMAs.
6. **`macdh` (MACD Histogram):** Identifies early directional shifts and deceleration in momentum before MACD signal line crossovers occur.
7. **`boll_ub` (Bollinger Upper Band):** Identifies upper volatility boundaries, overextension zones, and potential breakout continuations.
8. **`vwma` (Volume-Weighted Moving Average):** Incorporates trading volume into moving average analysis to measure institutional conviction behind price movements.
