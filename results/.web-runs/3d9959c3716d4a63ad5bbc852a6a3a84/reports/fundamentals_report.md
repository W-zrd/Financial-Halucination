## 1. Executive Summary

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company Limited (`TSM`) demonstrates sustained financial strength, operating efficiency, and market expansion across its trailing five quarterly reporting periods ending June 30, 2026. As the world’s preeminent pure-play semiconductor foundry, TSMC continues to capture outsized market share in advanced node manufacturing (3nm, 5nm/4nm) and advanced packaging solutions (CoWoS), propelled by durable high-performance computing (HPC) and artificial intelligence (AI) silicon demand.

Key fundamental takeaways include:
* **Accelerating Top-Line Growth:** Revenue expanded from NT$ 933.79 billion in Q2 2025 to NT$ 1,270.38 billion in Q2 2026, representing a +36.04% Year-over-Year (YoY) increase and a +12.02% Quarter-over-Quarter (QoQ) sequential increase.
* **Margin Expansion:** Gross profit margins widened dramatically from 58.62% in Q2 2025 to **67.72%** in Q2 2026. Operating margins exceeded 60% in Q2 2026 (60.35%), underscoring pricing power, scale efficiencies, and high capacity utilization.
* **Earnings Surge:** Quarterly Net Income grew to NT$ 706.56 billion (Diluted EPS of NT$ 136.25 per share) in Q2 2026, up from NT$ 398.27 billion in Q2 2025. Normalized Net Income reached NT$ 653.13 billion when excluding a one-time gain of NT$ 63.20 billion on business disposition.
* **Fortress Balance Sheet:** Cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments totaled **NT$ 3,597.44 billion** (~$110+ billion USD) against total debt of NT$ 1,064.95 billion, yielding a net cash position of NT$ 2,532.49 billion.
* **Aggressive CapEx Deployment:** Capital expenditures rose to NT$ 505.45 billion in Q2 2026 to support global fab expansions and next-generation 2nm (N2) ramp-ups, while still generating NT$ 274.97 billion in Free Cash Flow (FCF).

## 2. Income Statement & Profitability Analysis

### Sequential & YoY Revenue Trajectory
TSMC's quarterly revenue trajectory reflects strong sequential demand for leading-edge node technologies:
* **Q2 2025:** NT$ 933.79B
* **Q3 2025:** NT$ 989.92B (+6.01% QoQ)
* **Q4 2025:** NT$ 1,046.09B (+5.67% QoQ)
* **Q1 2026:** NT$ 1,134.10B (+8.41% QoQ)
* **Q2 2026:** NT$ 1,270.38B (+12.02% QoQ; +36.04% YoY)

### Margin Profile & Cost Structure
* **Gross Profitability:** Cost of Revenue rose moderately from NT$ 386.42B in Q2 2025 to NT$ 410.07B in Q2 2026 despite revenue growing by over NT$ 336B. Consequently, Gross Profit reached **NT$ 860.31 billion** in Q2 2026, boosting gross margin to 67.72%.
* **Operating Efficiency:** Total operating expenses grew to NT$ 93.74 billion in Q2 2026 (7.38% of revenue), down from 8.99% of revenue in Q2 2025. 
* **Research & Development (R&D):** TSMC expanded R&D investments to NT$ 73.15 billion in Q2 2026 (up from NT$ 61.28 billion in Q2 2025), preserving its technology leadership in sub-3nm nodes, gate-all-around (GAA) N2 architecture, and monolithic/heterogeneous packaging integration.
* **Operating Profit:** Operating income reached NT$ 766.60 billion in Q2 2026, delivering an operating margin of **60.35%** vs. 49.63% in Q2 2025.

### Earnings & One-Off Items
* **Normalized vs. Reported Net Income:** Net income attributable to common shareholders stood at NT$ 706.56 billion in Q2 2026. This includes a Special Income Charge/Gain on Sale of Business of NT$ 63.20 billion. Normalized net income excluding unusual items came in at **NT$ 653.13 billion**, showing true underlying operational earnings growth of +14.26% QoQ from Q1 2026 (NT$ 571.61B) and +66.12% YoY from Q2 2025 (NT$ 393.17B).
* **Net Interest Income:** Interest income consistently outpaced interest expense (NT$ 30.04 billion interest income vs. NT$ 3.09 billion interest expense in Q2 2026), generating NT$ 26.96 billion in net interest income.

## 3. Balance Sheet & Financial Solvency

### Capital Structure & Solvency
TSMC’s balance sheet reflects capital stability and operational scale:
* **Total Assets:** Expanded to **NT$ 9,375.65 billion** as of June 30, 2026, up from NT$ 7,006.35 billion in June 2025.
* **Current Assets:** Reached **NT$ 4,565.70 billion**, backed by NT$ 3,134.22 billion in cash & cash equivalents, NT$ 463.22 billion in short-term financial investments, and NT$ 435.76 billion in net accounts receivable.
* **Working Capital:** Grew to **NT$ 2,707.94 billion** in Q2 2026 (Current Assets of NT$ 4,565.70B vs. Current Liabilities of NT$ 1,857.76B), representing a current ratio of **2.46x**.

### Asset Base Expansion
* **Property, Plant & Equipment (PPE):** Gross PPE crossed NT$ 10.04 trillion (NT$ 10,041.30 billion). Net PPE reached NT$ 4,357.78 billion.
* **Construction in Progress (CIP):** CIP stood at **NT$ 1,032.95 billion** in Q2 2026 (down sequentially from NT$ 1,741.97 billion in Q1 2026 as facilities transitioned into operational tools), reflecting active global site development across Taiwan, Arizona, Kumamoto, and Dresden.

### Debt Dynamics & Net Cash Position
* **Total Debt:** NT$ 1,064.95 billion (Long-Term Debt of NT$ 864.26 billion; Current Debt of NT$ 167.41 billion; Capital Leases of NT$ 33.28 billion).
* **Net Cash Reserve:** Excess cash over total debt stands at **NT$ 2,532.49 billion**, mitigating interest rate risks and ensuring organic funding for upcoming CapEx cycles without liquidity strain.

## 4. Cash Flow Analysis & Capital Allocation

### Cash Flow Generation
* **Operating Cash Flow (OCF):** Delivered **NT$ 783.36 billion** in Q2 2026, up +57.60% YoY from NT$ 497.06 billion in Q2 2025.
* **Capital Expenditures (CapEx):** CapEx (Purchase of PPE) reached **NT$ 505.45 billion** in Q2 2026, expanding from NT$ 350.77 billion in Q1 2026 and NT$ 297.23 billion in Q2 2025. Total TTM CapEx surpassed NT$ 1.50 trillion.
* **Free Cash Flow (FCF):** Despite heavy CapEx, FCF remained positive at **NT$ 274.97 billion** in Q2 2026, bringing 4-quarter cumulative FCF to over **NT$ 1.12 trillion**.

### Shareholder Distributions
* **Dividend Growth:** Cash dividend payments expanded from NT$ 116.70 billion in Q2 2025 to **NT$ 155.60 billion** in Q2 2026. Dividends payable stood at NT$ 337.44 billion at quarter-end, reflecting TSMC's policy of sustainable, rising cash dividends tied to FCF expansion.
