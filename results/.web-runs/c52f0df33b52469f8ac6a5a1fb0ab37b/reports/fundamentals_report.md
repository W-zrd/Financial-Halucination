## 6. Actionable Insights for Traders

1. **Momentum in fundamentals is exceptionally strong:** Revenue growth re-accelerated (QoQ +33%, YoY +86%), with margins expanding and FCF near record levels. This supports the AI/infrastructure-software thesis and a premium valuation.
2. **Watch the deleveraging story:** Net debt dropping from ~$52B to ~$35B in two quarters lowers interest expense (interest expense was ~$778M/quarter and falling) and frees capital for future buybacks or M&A. This is a tailwind at a time when rates may be less favorable.
3. **Tax-rate volatility** distorts GAAP net income quarter-to-quarter (tax provision ranged from -$1.65B to +$2.19B across recent quarters). Normalized EBITDA ($18.4B in Q3) is a cleaner read on operating momentum than GAAP EPS.
4. **Balance sheet quality caveat:** Negative tangible book value means traditional book-value-based valuation is meaningless; investors are paying for intangibles/franchise value and cash flow, not hard assets.
5. **Insider selling** (heavy officer/founder sales vs. essentially no open-market buys) is the main fundamental "yellow flag" — worth monitoring for any acceleration, though it does not appear to be breaking the trend of routine selling.
6. **Dividend safety is high:** ~4x FCF coverage and a still-small payout means the dividend growth streak (dividends rose from ~$9.8B in FY24 to $11.1B in FY25) has room to continue.
