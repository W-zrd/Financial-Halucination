## 1. Selected Technical Indicators & Rationale

To deliver a multifaceted assessment without redundancy, eight complementary indicators across trend, momentum, volatility, and volume were evaluated:

1. **close_200_sma (200 SMA):** Establishes the macro trend direction and primary structural support baseline.
2. **close_50_sma (50 SMA):** Identifies intermediate-term trend structure and key dynamic support/resistance.
3. **close_10_ema (10 EMA):** Gauges short-term trend responsiveness and tracks tactical pullbacks or momentum acceleration.
4. **macd (Moving Average Convergence Divergence):** Measures trend strength and moving-average divergence.
5. **macdh (MACD Histogram):** Provides an early warning mechanism for changes in momentum direction and speed.
6. **rsi (Relative Strength Index - 14 period):** Assesses overbought/oversold conditions and intermediate momentum balance.
7. **boll (Bollinger Bands - boll, boll_ub, boll_lb):** Quantifies dynamic volatility ranges and defines overextended envelope boundaries.
8. **atr (Average True Range - 14 period):** Captures true daily trading range volatility for setting volatility-adjusted stops and targets.

*(Note: Volume-weighted confirmation via **vwma** was also observed to monitor volume-weighted distribution).*
