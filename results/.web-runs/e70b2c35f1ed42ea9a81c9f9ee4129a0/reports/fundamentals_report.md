# Fundamental Analysis Report: Walmart Inc. (NYSE: WMT)
**Analysis Date:** September 25, 2026  
**Instrument:** `WMT` | **Exchange:** NMS | **Sector/Industry:** Consumer Defensive / Discount Stores  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Investment Thesis

Walmart Inc. (`WMT`) demonstrates resilient fundamental momentum driven by market share gains across grocery and general merchandise, acceleration in high-margin non-retail revenue streams (Walmart Connect retail media, e-commerce marketplace, and data monetization), and continuous operating efficiency through supply chain automation. 

In the most recent quarter (Q2 FY2027, period ended July 31, 2026), Walmart delivered **$187.94 billion** in total revenue (+5.94% YoY) and **$9.38 billion** in operating income (+28.78% YoY), reflecting strong operating leverage as gross margins expanded to 26.14%. Free cash flow reached **$7.48 billion** for the quarter, supporting sustained capital reinvestment ($7.50B quarterly CapEx) and accelerated capital returns to shareholders via **$3.02 billion** in quarterly share repurchases and **$1.97 billion** in dividends.

---

## 2. Income Statement & Earnings Quality Analysis

### A. Revenue Growth & Top-Line Trends
* **Quarterly Revenue Progression:**
  * **Q2 FY2027 (ended 2026-07-31):** $187.94B (+5.94% YoY vs. $177.40B in Q2 FY2026; +5.73% QoQ).
  * **Q1 FY2027 (ended 2026-04-30):** $177.75B (+4.5% YoY).
  * **Q4 FY2026 (ended 2026-01-31):** $190.66B (Holiday quarter).
  * **Q3 FY2026 (ended 2025-10-31):** $179.50B.
  * **Trailing Twelve Months (TTM) Revenue:** **$735.85B**, reinforcing Walmart’s dominant global scale.

### B. Profitability & Operating Margins
* **Gross Profit:** Reached **$49.13B** in Q2 FY2027 (26.14% gross margin), expanding from 25.16% in Q2 FY2026 (+98 bps YoY). Gross margin expansion has been powered by reduced net markdowns, supply chain efficiencies, and higher-margin digital advertising contributions.
* **Operating Income:** Grew **28.78% YoY to $9.38B** (operating margin of 4.99%, up from 4.11% in Q2 FY2026).
* **Operating Expenses:** Stood at $39.75B (21.15% of revenue), remaining tightly managed relative to revenue growth.
* **Net Income & EPS:**
  * **Net Income to Common Stockholders:** $6.37B in Q2 FY2027, delivering **Diluted EPS of $0.80** (compared to $0.67 in Q1 FY2027 and $0.53 in Q4 FY2026).
  * Net income included an unrealized mark-to-market/other non-operating expense of -$1.20B (vs. +$2.71B gain in the prior year period), demonstrating that core operating earnings strength was even more pronounced than the GAAP headline net income suggests.

## 3. Balance Sheet Health & Liquidity Structure

### A. Working Capital & Asset Quality
* **Cash & Equivalents:** Maintained healthy liquidity of **$11.53B** as of July 31, 2026, up from $9.43B a year prior.
* **Inventory Management:** Total inventory was **$61.60B**, down sequentially from $62.57B in April 2026 and well-balanced (+6.7% YoY) relative to ~6% top-line growth, signaling clean inventory composition and minimal obsolescence risk.
* **Working Capital Dynamics:** Working capital stood at **-$26.93B**, typical for a world-class retail operator running an efficient negative working capital cash-conversion cycle (leveraging $64.32B in accounts payable with high inventory turnover).
* **Property, Plant & Equipment (PPE):** Net PPE expanded to **$164.31B** (vs. $145.56B in Q2 FY2026), reflecting ongoing investments in automated distribution centers, market fulfillment centers, and store fleet updates.

### B. Solvency & Debt Profile
* **Total Debt:** $73.76B (comprising $36.46B long-term debt, $23.34B capital lease obligations, and $13.95B short-term/current borrowings).
* **Net Debt:** **$38.88B** (Total debt less cash & equivalents and short-term capital lease adjustments).
* **Equity & Leverage:** Stockholders’ equity increased to **$98.24B** (+9.02% YoY from $90.11B). Interest coverage remains exceptionally safe, with quarterly EBIT of $8.28B covering net interest expense ($171M) by over 48x.

---

## 4. Cash Flow & Capital Allocation

### A. Cash Flow from Operations & Free Cash Flow
* **Operating Cash Flow (OCF):** Surged to **$14.97B** in Q2 FY2027, compared to $12.94B in Q2 FY2026 (+15.70% YoY), driven by strong cash earnings and positive working capital inflows of $2.70B.
* **Capital Expenditures (CapEx):** $7.50B in Q2 FY2027 ($29.41B over the last four quarters). CapEx remains focused on technology, automated fulfillment, and omni-channel retail infrastructure.
* **Free Cash Flow (FCF):** Generated **$7.48B** in Q2 FY2027 (+14.68% YoY from $6.52B), providing robust self-funding for capital return programs.

### B. Shareholder Returns
* **Share Repurchases:** Walmart ramped up its share buyback program, deploying **$3.02B** in Q2 FY2027 (up from $2.08B in Q1 FY2027 and $1.08B in Q4 FY2026). Total TTM share buybacks totaled **$6.99B**.
* **Dividends Paid:** Consistently returned **$1.97B** per quarter in dividends (~$7.70B annualized).
* **Share Count Reduction:** Diluted shares outstanding decreased from 8.016 billion to 7.978 billion.

  * No unusual, clustered, or uncharacteristic discretionary insider selling detected outside standard 10b5-1 selling schedules.

## 6. Actionable Insights for Traders

1. **Defensive Growth & Flight to Quality:** Walmart offers superior defensive characteristics with consistent 5–6% top-line growth. It benefits from consumer trade-down behavior during macroeconomic uncertainty while sustaining middle-to-upper-income demographic gains.
2. **Structural Margin Expansion:** Operating income growing at >25% YoY significantly outpaces revenue growth (~6%), confirming structural margin tailwinds from high-margin digital advertising (Walmart Connect) and fulfillment services.
3. **Catalyst Watch:**
   * **Holiday Build & Omni-Channel Execution:** Continued inventory discipline entering Q3/Q4 holiday build periods.
   * **Capital Return Acceleration:** Substantial FCF generation ($7.5B/quarter) provides upside potential for further share repurchases and dividend growth.
4. **Trading Strategy Consideration:** Buy-on-dips strategy supported by strong fundamental floor, safe balance sheet coverage, and steady capital return yield.
