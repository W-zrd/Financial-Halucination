## Assessment

Note the ATR of 25.5 means a single bad session can cover much of that distance — size positions accordingly.

Given the flat 50 SMA through the March crash, this looks more like a broad uptrend that had a sharp correction and is now reasserting, rather than a dead-cat bounce off a broken trend.
