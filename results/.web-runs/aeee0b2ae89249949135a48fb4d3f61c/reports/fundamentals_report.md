### 3.2 The tax noise — do not be misled by reported EPS

Two enormous, offsetting tax items dominate reported earnings:

- **FQ4 FY2025 (Sep-2025):** tax provision of **$6,088M** on $2,971M of pretax income → reported net loss of **-$3,117M** (EPS -$2.89).

**This is the most actionable cash-flow finding in the report:**

**Sustainability question:** If the FQ3'26 run-rate (FCF $495M/quarter) persisted, the current dividend (~$950M/quarter) plus buyback would be materially underfunded. Management would have to either cut buybacks, reduce the dividend growth rate, or accept continued balance-sheet drawdown. **Watch the FY2026 Q4 report for any change in the buyback pace — that is the tell.**

## 10. Key Risks

1. **Negative operating leverage is real and worsening.** Opex +22% YoY against revenue -4% YoY in FQ3'26. If this continues, operating margin has further to fall from 16.3%.
2. **Gross margin erosion appears structural so far** — four straight quarters of decline (55.6% → 53.1%). Watch for a stabilization signal; none is present in the data.
3. **Free cash flow collapse.** FQ3'26 FCF of $495M (-81% YoY) against ~$950M of quarterly dividends is not sustainable without balance-sheet drawdown.
4. **Inventory build of +28% in three quarters** into declining revenue — obsolescence/write-down and further gross-margin risk.
5. **Capital returns exceed cash generation (126% of TTM FCF).** Cash fell $5.0B and net debt rose $4.0B over the TTM period. A buyback slowdown or a slower dividend growth rate is a plausible near-term consequence.
6. **Refinancing requirement:** $2,489M of debt is now current (up from $498M), coinciding with the cash drain.
7. **Earnings-quality/optics risk:** massive one-off tax swings ($6,088M charge then $5,138M benefit) plus ~$972M of non-operating investment gains make headline GAAP numbers unreliable and create headline risk around the FY2026 print.
8. **Imminent catalyst risk:** FY2026 ends 2026-09-30, five days after this analysis date, with results historically reported in early November. Given the FQ3 trajectory, guidance risk is elevated.
9. **Insider alignment:** unanimous, persistent selling with zero purchases.
10. **Elevated expectations:** at ~23x adjusted GAAP earnings / ~20x FCF for a flat-to-declining top line, the stock is not priced for the current fundamental trend.

## 11. Positives / Offsetting Factors

1. **Still a high-quality, highly profitable franchise:** 54.2% TTM gross margin, 23.3% TTM operating margin, 30.6% EBITDA margin.
2. **Very low leverage:** 0.80x net debt/EBITDA, ~14.9x interest coverage. Balance-sheet stress is not the issue — cash-flow direction is.
3. **Large absolute cash generation:** $10.4B TTM free cash flow, $12.4B TTM operating cash flow.
4. **FY2025 was an operational success:** revenue +13.7% YoY to $44.3B, operating income +21% YoY. The FY2025 GAAP weakness was purely tax-driven.
5. **Attractive shareholder yield:** ~6.4% combined (1.9% dividend + 4.5% buyback), with diluted shares down 2.7% YoY.
6. **Rising investment:** R&D +17% YoY and capex +66% YoY signal a genuine investment cycle — potentially the cause of current margin pressure and the source of future growth, if it converts.
7. **Diversification optionality (contextual):** as a semiconductor designer, QCOM's long-term narrative rests on expanding beyond handsets into auto, IoT, PC and edge-AI — which is plausibly what the stepped-up R&D and M&A (goodwill +$2.8B, $1.6B TTM business purchases) are funding. **However, none of that is yet visible in the revenue line, which is flat.**

## 12. Actionable Insights for Traders

**The central tension:** QCOM is a cash-rich, high-margin, low-leverage compounder with a ~6.4% shareholder yield — trading at a full multiple while every forward-looking fundamental indicator in the last four quarters points the wrong way.

**Specific, evidence-backed observations:**

1. **The trend is unambiguously negative on fundamentals.** Revenue -4% YoY, gross margin -250bps over four quarters, operating margin -1,030bps YoY, FCF -81% YoY. Any long thesis requires a specific, evidence-based reason to believe this is a trough — and the tools provide none.
2. **The FY2026 Q4 print (early November, fiscal year ends 2026-09-30) is the pivotal catalyst.** The three things to check, in priority order:
   - **Gross margin:** does it stabilize above 53% or continue lower?
   - **Buyback pace:** does the company sustain $2.5B+/quarter, or step down given FCF of $495M?
   - **Inventory:** does the $8.4B balance come down or get written down?
3. **The CEO sold 10,000 shares at $195.00 on 2026-09-21, four days ago, days before fiscal year-end.** With the COO selling in eight of the last nine months and **zero insider purchases across the entire dataset**, insider behavior offers no support for the long case.
4. **Positioning into the print is asymmetric to the downside on the numbers as they stand** — the stock is in the upper portion of its six-month range (~$195 vs a ~$130–227 band) while fundamentals are at their weakest. A long here is a bet on a turnaround that is not yet visible; a short/underweight has a well-defined fundamental rationale but faces a strong balance sheet, a ~6.4% yield, and the risk of a positive guidance surprise.
5. **The ~126% FCF payout ratio is the most under-appreciated risk.** If FCF stays near the FQ3'26 run-rate, the market will have to re-price either the dividend, the buyback, or the debt trajectory. Buyback reduction would remove a key support for EPS growth (share count fell 2.7% YoY and is a meaningful part of per-share results).
